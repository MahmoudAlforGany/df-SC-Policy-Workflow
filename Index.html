Decision Gates · Hard Stops
CHAPTER 13 · 13.6
Spec Completeness Gate
No medical / recovery RFQ floats without all 8 spec fields confirmed by BDU. Generic descriptions (e.g. "Shockwave machine") fail the gate.
Validator: BDU · Technical Owner
CHAPTER 6 · 6.3.2.A
TS Site Readiness
Riyadh / Khobar shipments must carry written Technical Support approval before warehouse releases. Wared dwell maxes at 14 days then auto-returns.
Validator: TS Manager
CHAPTER 10 · 10.3.1
RAF Dual Approval
Return Authorization Form requires BOTH Technical Manager AND SC Manager sign-off. Warehouse refuses any return arriving without prior dual approval.
Validators: TS Mgr + SC Mgr
CHAPTER 12 · 12.5
CEO Write-off Gate
Items aged > 1,080 days with 6 months no sales activity require CEO approval, written SC justification, Finance audit trail, and physical count.
Validator: CEO · Finance trail
